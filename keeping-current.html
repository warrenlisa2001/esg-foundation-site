Keeping Current

ESG news, governance and disclosure — as it happens.

Thirteen stories at a time from the Foundation's own newsroom: what's changing in disclosure, governance, climate and social reporting, plus our own announcements. Free to read, no login required.

Governance

UAE Formalises ESG Disclosure Metrics as Sustainable Bond Issuance Hits a Record AED 28.6 Billion

ADX's new ESG disclosure guidance standardises around 40 reporting metrics as UAE sustainable bond and sukuk issuance reached a record AED 28.6 billion in 2025.

Foundation

ESG Foundation Adds 40 New Reports to the Library This Quarter

The free public library now spans ten industries, with the largest quarterly intake since launch.

Governance

Board Oversight of ESG Now Disclosed by Most GCC-Listed Majors, Benchmark Finds

A growing share of large-cap disclosure now names the board committee responsible for sustainability oversight, GCC ESG Benchmark data shows.

Climate

Scope 3 Reporting Still Lags Behind Scope 1 and 2, Library Analysis Shows

Most reports in the library now disclose direct and energy emissions in full. Value-chain emissions remain the exception.

Foundation

ESG Foundation Opens Nominations for the 2026 ESG Foundation Awards

The annual programme recognises credible, well-evidenced ESG disclosure and practice. Nominations are free and open to any organisation in the library.

Social

Supply-Chain Labour Audits Becoming Standard Practice Among Large Manufacturers

Third-party labour audits of tier-one suppliers now appear in a majority of manufacturing-sector reports reviewed for the library.

Governance

Materiality Matrices Rarely Disclose Their Own Methodology, Library Review Finds

Nearly every report in the library includes a materiality matrix. Few explain how the underlying stakeholder engagement was actually conducted.

Climate

Biodiversity Disclosure Moves From Niche to Mainstream in the 2026 Reporting Cycle

Nature and biodiversity sections, once confined to extractives and agriculture, now appear across a much wider range of library submissions.

Foundation

ESG Foundation Opens the GCC Benchmark Methodology for Public Comment

The Foundation is inviting feedback on its scoring methodology ahead of the next Benchmark update, in keeping with its full-traceability principle.

Governance

CSRD-Style Reporting Requirements Continue to Expand Beyond the EU

Reports submitted to the library increasingly reference double-materiality concepts even outside jurisdictions where they are formally required.

Social

Gender Pay Gap Reporting Reaches Mid-Sized Employers in Three New Markets

Pay-gap disclosure, once concentrated among the largest listed employers, is appearing further down the size distribution this reporting cycle.

Governance

Executive Pay-for-ESG-Performance Links Become More Common, Still Rarely Explained

More remuneration reports now mention ESG metrics in incentive design. Few disclose the actual weighting or targets used.

Foundation

The ESG Podcast Passes 25 Episodes With a Focus on GCC Disclosure Practice

Recent episodes have turned toward practitioners actually building ESG functions inside GCC-listed companies, rather than the conference-panel circuit.

ESG Foundation

ESG Foundation is a non-profit knowledge base for environmental, social and governance data. We aggregate disclosures, filings and public reporting from across the ESG field, publish the method we use to do it, and make the record free to read. Where data does not exist, we publish the gap.
