Awards

The ESG Foundation Awards

An annual programme recognising credible, well-evidenced ESG disclosure and practice across the region. Six categories, judged on published reports alone. Nomination is free, always.

Submit a nomination Ask a question

Categories

Six categories, one standard: evidence.

Best First-Time Disclosure

For an organisation publishing its first standalone sustainability or ESG report.

Governance Reporting Award

For clear, decision-useful disclosure of board oversight and accountability.

Climate Transition Plan Award

For a credible, well-evidenced pathway to stated emissions targets.

Social & Workforce Reporting Award

For disclosure that gives a genuine, checkable picture of how people are treated.

Traceability & Data Quality Award

For reporting where every figure is easy to locate, define and verify at source.

GCC Newcomer of the Year

For the strongest debut appearance in the GCC ESG Benchmark.

How it works

Nominated, reviewed, announced — no fee at any step.

1. Nominate

Self-nominate or nominate another organisation. One published report, freely accessible, is all that's required.

2. Independent review

A judging panel drawn from outside the Foundation reviews each nomination against the published report alone.

3. Announced

Winners are announced publicly, alongside the reasoning behind each result.

As with Research and the Benchmark, no organisation pays to be nominated, shortlisted, or to attend. The Foundation does not accept sponsorship of individual award categories.

Nominations are open.

Free to enter. Self-nominate, or put forward another organisation.

ESG Foundation

ESG Foundation is a non-profit knowledge base for environmental, social and governance data. We aggregate disclosures, filings and public reporting from across the ESG field, publish the method we use to do it, and make the record free to read. Where data does not exist, we publish the gap.
