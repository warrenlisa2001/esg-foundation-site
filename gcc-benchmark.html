The method is part of the record.

We publish how information is collected, assessed and treated. That includes the places where evidence is incomplete, estimated or disputed.

Read the method

Four statuses. No ambiguity.

VERIFIED SOURCE Evidence exists and is linked to the record.
ESTIMATED A value has been estimated and is clearly identified.
DISPUTED The available evidence or interpretation is contested.
NO DATA The required public evidence does not exist.

The open record for ESG data.

ESG Foundation is a non-profit knowledge base for environmental, social and governance data. We aggregate disclosures, filings and public reporting from across the ESG field, publish the method we use to do it, and make the record free to read. Where data does not exist, we publish the gap.
