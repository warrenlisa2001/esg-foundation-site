Submit a report

Inclusion is free — every submission is reviewed first.

There is no fee, no questionnaire and no membership. A member of our team verifies each submission before it goes live; we do not accept AI-generated report summaries, descriptions or cover images.

Verified, not AI-written

The report and its listing description must be prepared and checked by your organisation, not generated by AI.

A short title & summary

A short, plain-language title and 1–2 sentence description we can use for the listing and its meta tags.

High-resolution cover image

A professional, high-res image (at least 1200px wide) for the report's card and social preview.

References & backlinks

Sources, data references and any third-party assurance cited in the report, with links where available.

Industry, region & year

Tag your report's industry, region and reporting year so it's placed in the right section of Research.

A link to the published report

A direct PDF or webpage link so readers can view or download the full report.

Ready to submit?

Email us the details above and our team will review, verify and publish it — typically within a few business days.

ESG Foundation

ESG Foundation is a non-profit knowledge base for environmental, social and governance data. We aggregate disclosures, filings and public reporting from across the ESG field, publish the method we use to do it, and make the record free to read. Where data does not exist, we publish the gap.
