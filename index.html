WHY THIS EXISTS

ESG data is not difficult because there is no data.

It is difficult because the record is fragmented, inconsistent and often hidden behind commercial access.

01

We aggregate the record.

Disclosures, filings, public reporting, regulatory material, standards and other published evidence are brought into one searchable knowledge base.

02

We publish the method.

The Foundation shows how information is sourced, classified and recorded. The method is part of the record, not a black box.

03

We show the gaps.

Where a company, supplier or indicator has no public disclosure, that absence is recorded rather than filled with an assumption.

04

We keep evidence visible.

Every figure carries a status and a source trail so readers can distinguish verified information, estimates, disputes and gaps.

ABOUT ESG FOUNDATION

An open record for the ESG field.

ESG Foundation is a non-profit knowledge base for environmental, social and governance data. We aggregate disclosures, filings and public reporting from across the ESG field, publish the method we use to do it, and make the record free to read. Where data does not exist, we publish the gap.
