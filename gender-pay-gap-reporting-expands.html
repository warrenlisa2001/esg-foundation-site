Gender Pay Gap Reporting Reaches Mid-Sized Employers in Three New Markets

August 18, 2026 · ESG Foundation

Gender pay-gap disclosure, historically concentrated among the largest listed employers, has started appearing in reports from notably smaller organisations across three markets not previously well represented in the library.

Methodology still varies significantly — some report a simple mean gap, others a full quartile breakdown — which the Foundation notes makes cross-company comparison difficult without reading the underlying methodology note.
