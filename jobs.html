Working here

A small team, a public mission.

The Foundation runs lean by design — the record, the Benchmark and Keeping Current are built and maintained by a handful of people working between London and Dubai, not a large newsroom or ratings house. That means broad remits: most people here work across research, data and communications rather than a single narrow lane.

The work is public by default. What gets built and published is the product — there's no sales team, no client-facing scoring, and no commercial pressure on what a report is found to say.

What we look for

The kinds of skills the team draws on.

Research & ESG analysis

Reading disclosure closely — methodology, materiality, what a figure actually supports.

Data & engineering

Building and maintaining the extraction and traceability tooling behind the Benchmark.

Government & stakeholder relations

Connecting the Foundation's methodology work to regulators, exchanges and policy conversations across the GCC.

Communications & editorial

Keeping Current, the podcast, and making disclosure legible to a non-specialist reader.

ESG Foundation

ESG Foundation is a non-profit knowledge base for environmental, social and governance data. We aggregate disclosures, filings and public reporting from across the ESG field, publish the method we use to do it, and make the record free to read. Where data does not exist, we publish the gap.
