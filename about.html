About Us

The public reading room for sustainability disclosure.

Founded in 2020, ESG Foundation exists to focus the attention of organisations on a straightforward proposition: that sound environmental, social and governance behaviour is vital to the wellbeing of the stakeholders they serve.

What we do

We curate a free public showcase of ESG and sustainability reporting, publish a disclosure benchmark for companies listed in Abu Dhabi and Dubai with every figure traced to its source, and convene practitioners through a podcast and an awards programme. We do not issue questionnaires, charge organisations to be included, or rate or certify anyone.

How it's funded

The Showcase, the Benchmark and the underlying methodology are free to access. No company pays to be included, excluded, corrected or ranked. Data-extraction technology is licensed from Anthropogenic AI on ordinary commercial terms.

Leadership

A Board of Directors oversees the Foundation's published work and the independence of its methods. A Board of Advisors, currently being formed, advises on methodology and holds no executive authority; all advisor positions are unpaid, two-year terms.

Chairman

Ivano Iannelli

Chairman of ESG Foundation and CEO of TRST01, Ivano Iannelli brings two decades connecting sustainability economics to commercial performance — including as former sustainability lead on a USD 250 million decarbonisation fund. His work across the GCC and MENA grounds the Foundation's methodology in what actually moves capital, not just what satisfies a checklist.

“Sustainability is not a reporting function. It is the architecture of long-term competitive advantage — deeply rooted in strategy, not marketing.”
Director, AI & Data Systems

Lisa Warren

Lisa Warren leads the technology behind the Foundation's disclosure engine — the agentic AI systems that extract, trace and verify every figure back to the page it came from. Her background in autonomous agent systems and enterprise AI orchestration is what makes full traceability possible at the scale the Showcase and Benchmark operate.

“Disclosure only matters when you can trace every figure back to where it came from — that's what we built the extraction engine to do.”
Director, Government Relations

Ayesha Alafeefi

Ayesha Alafeefi leads the Foundation's engagement with regulators, exchanges and government stakeholders across the GCC. With over 14 years representing the UAE at forums including G20 and BRICS Tourism Ministerials, the UN Tourism Executive Council, the Arab League, GCC and OIC, she connects the Foundation's methodology work to the policy conversations shaping disclosure requirements across the region.

“Translating complex regulatory objectives into measurable disclosure outcomes is what fourteen years of working alongside ministers and international stakeholders has taught me to do well.”
