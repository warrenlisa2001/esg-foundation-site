How funding works

Support pays for access. It doesn't buy influence.

Supporter funding covers the cost of running a free public library, an extraction and traceability engine, and a small editorial team — nothing more. No organisation pays to be included in, excluded from, corrected in, or ranked by Research or the Benchmark, whether or not it supports the Foundation. Methodology decisions sit with the Foundation's own team, informed but not directed by the Board of Advisors.

“If a supporter's money could move a score, the score would be worthless — and so would everything built on top of it. Independence isn't a value statement here, it's the product.”

Ivano Iannelli, Chairman

ESG Foundation

ESG Foundation is a non-profit knowledge base for environmental, social and governance data. We aggregate disclosures, filings and public reporting from across the ESG field, publish the method we use to do it, and make the record free to read. Where data does not exist, we publish the gap.
